# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Switchboard project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take appropriate corrective action in response to behavior they determine is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. Reasons for moderation decisions may be communicated when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Conduct reports may be submitted through an appropriate GitHub moderation or private maintainer channel when one is available.

Do not include private personal information, credentials, vulnerability details, or exploit instructions in a public report. Security-sensitive matters must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use private GitHub Security Advisory reporting when available.

Maintainers will review reports as availability allows and will aim to handle them respectfully and fairly. No fixed response timeline is guaranteed.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will use these Community Impact Guidelines when determining consequences for behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Inappropriate language or behavior considered unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private or public warning that explains the issue and why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. Maintainers may limit interaction within project spaces for a specified period. Further violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from interaction within project spaces. Further violations may result in a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** A pattern of violations, sustained harassment, aggression, or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from interaction within project spaces.
